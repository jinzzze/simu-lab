Separate learned components

Macro-outcome model: 28.41 mm mean endpoint error and 153/160 correct success/failure labels across 32 test initial scenes. This is prediction accuracy; only 71/160 recorded actions succeeded. The model does not select actions.

Compact DP-D: 84/192 strict successes (27, 25 and 32 out of 64 per seed), with 128 additional simulated expert training trajectories. Initial estimated XY, robot state and time condition the policy; it is not an end-to-end RGB-history benchmark replica. Two rendered-image overlaps are disclosed; exclusion sensitivity is 80/186.

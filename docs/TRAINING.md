# Training from frozen personal inputs

Use the **full v1.1.0 evidence ZIP**. The quick replay ZIP intentionally omits training data. The release preserves recorded outputs; training must use a separate new workspace. No additional training is needed to inspect the submitted results.

## Prepare a new workspace

From the full package root, using the environment installed as described in [QUICKSTART](QUICKSTART.md):

```powershell
. ./scripts/Enter-Project.ps1
& ./.env/python.exe -B scripts/verify_timing_package.py
& ./.env/python.exe -B scripts/prepare_timing_rerun.py --output ../simu-lab-training-repeat01
```

The preparation script verifies the personal-input hashes, copies frozen sources/configuration/profiles into a destination that must not exist, and copies no trained models, robot demonstrations or evaluation outputs. It does not launch training. It preserves the frozen split and goal geometry. `RERUN_INPUTS.json` lists the copied files.

Open the new directory and install its environment using the same quickstart commands. Then:

```powershell
. ./scripts/Enter-Project.ps1
& ./.env/python.exe -B experiments/personal_video_timing_v4/test_study_boundaries.py
& ./.env/python.exe -B -u experiments/personal_video_timing_v4/collect.py
& ./.env/python.exe -B -u experiments/personal_video_timing_v4/run_pipeline.py
& ./.env/python.exe -B experiments/personal_video_timing_v4/report_results.py
```

Do not call `prepare_study.py` here: the study and personal profiles are already frozen. That script intentionally refuses to overwrite them.

Collection executes 24 teacher attempts per condition plus 12 shared validation attempts. Training is gated on 24 qualified lifts and at least 20 strict placements out of 24 in each condition. Failed demonstrations are retained. The pipeline trains all three seeds per condition for 2,500 updates, audits pairing, evaluates validation, freezes the six checkpoints and then evaluates the predeclared test cases. It preserves incomplete/failed revisions for investigation rather than silently deleting them.

Recorded learner training took approximately 123–133 seconds per model on the original RTX 3080 Ti Laptop GPU; that is **training only**, excluding video processing, demonstration collection, evaluation, installation and downloads. It is not an end-to-end runtime estimate for another computer. Exact full-retraining determinism and another-machine execution have not been established by the fixed-case replay checks.

## Video preprocessing provenance

`experiments/personal_video_timing_v4/data/processed/human_profiles_v1.json` records the 24 selected sources and hashes of raw recordings, calibration, frame candidates and reviewed coarse event intervals. S01 contributes eight clips; X01 contributes sixteen. X02/X03 never enter those profiles. The full package includes the recorded derivatives; their human-facing review brackets cannot be regenerated by pretending they are automatic physical ground truth.

Original extraction scripts remain under `experiments/paired_two_board_v2/` and `experiments/cross_goal_composition_v3/`. Recomputing candidates requires the documented external MediaPipe model, obtainable with `scripts/fetch_external_models.py`, which checks upstream hashes. Candidate coverage does not establish label accuracy. The comparison learns robot commands from physically executed simulation demonstrations, not from recovered human action labels.
